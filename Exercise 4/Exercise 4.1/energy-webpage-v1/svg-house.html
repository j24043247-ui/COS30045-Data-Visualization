STEP 2 · COORDINATE SYSTEM

Understanding SVG Coordinates

SVG coordinates start at the top-left corner. The x-coordinate increases from left to right, while the y-coordinate increases from top to bottom.

Example: the house body starts at (300, 245). Changing x moves the shape horizontally; changing y moves it vertically.

STEP 3 · CUSTOMISE YOUR PICTURE

STEP 4 · GROUP ELEMENT

Two Windows Using <g>

The two windows are placed inside one group so common styling can be applied to both. Translation is then used to position the second window.

<g id="windows" fill="#9bd7ee" stroke="#315c6b" stroke-width="6">
  <g transform="translate(0,0)">...first window...</g>
  <g transform="translate(235,0)">...second window...</g>
</g>
