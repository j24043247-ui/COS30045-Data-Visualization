Appliance Energy Consumption

Appliances make up an important part of household electricity use. Understanding an appliance's power rating and how long it operates can help households make more informed energy decisions.

⚡

Power Usage

Electrical appliances are commonly rated in watts. Higher wattage generally means more electricity is consumed while the appliance is operating.

◷

Usage Time

The amount of time an appliance is used also affects its total energy consumption. Reducing unnecessary operating time can lower electricity use.

▣

Energy Efficiency

Energy-efficient appliances can provide the same useful service while using less electricity, helping households manage long-term energy costs.

Appliance energy consumption describes the amount of electrical energy an appliance uses while operating. It is commonly measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh).

Wattage indicates the rate at which an electrical appliance uses power. A 1000-watt appliance uses power at a rate of 1 kilowatt while operating.

You can reduce energy use by choosing efficient appliances, avoiding unnecessary operation, using appropriate settings and switching appliances off when they are not required.

A kilowatt-hour is a unit of energy. It represents using 1 kilowatt of power continuously for 1 hour.
